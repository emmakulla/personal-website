01 / ABOUT ME

More than just numbers.

I love discovering the story behind the data—and finding ways to make an impact beyond the spreadsheet.

I'm Emma Kulla, a student at Northeastern University pursuing a combined degree in Data Science and Business Administration with a concentration in Finance. I enjoy studying how information can shape decisions, whether that means identifying a pattern in a dataset, learning how financial markets operate, or turning a complex question into a clear and useful visualization. My academic interests bring together analytical thinking, business strategy, and creativity, and I am excited to keep exploring where those fields overlap.

Outside the classroom, I value experiences that challenge me to learn from other people. My professional experiences have introduced me to financial operations, business processes, and the importance of careful analysis. At Fiduciary Trust Company, I worked in cash management and operations. During an internship at ASML, I explored inventory-related processes and collaborated with colleagues to understand how data could support improvements. These opportunities helped me appreciate how technical skills can contribute to practical, real-world problems.

Campus involvement is another important part of my experience. I serve as a Resident Assistant, enjoy planning community-focused events, and have been involved in community service and leadership through Delta Sigma Pi. I also founded Northeastern's Albanian Student Association to help celebrate culture and bring students together. Whether I'm working on a course project, preparing an event, or learning something completely new, I try to approach each opportunity with curiosity, collaboration, and a willingness to grow.
